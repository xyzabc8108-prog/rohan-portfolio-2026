01 / WHAT IT IS

THE CONCEPT.

This project explores financial-chart interfaces and analysis concepts. The publicly accessible version operates in Market Data Safety Mode, so it must not be described as a live stock-signal, prediction or brokerage product.

HONEST CREDIT The project idea and direction are mine. AI tools generated most of the implementation. My own testing has been limited and the project status is reported accordingly.

02 / CURRENT WORK

WHAT EXISTS.

  • A FastAPI-backed modular web interface for market research concepts and educational experiments.
  • A public synthetic demonstration with fictional internally generated sequences and descriptive indicators.
  • Safety restrictions for public data handling, uploaded inputs and stock-specific trade-level scenarios.

03 / EVIDENCE & LIMITATIONS

WHAT'S KNOWN.

  • The connected public repository documents Market Data Safety Mode and disabled public stock-specific recommendations.
  • The public site is hosted on Render; access or uptime can vary with the hosting service.
  • There is no verified real-time licensed exchange feed, no broker execution and no authorized live trading signals.
VISUAL EVIDENCE The illustration on this page is original portfolio artwork, not a screenshot. A verified, permission-cleared product screenshot or recording can be added later.

04 / WHAT COMES NEXT

STILL TO DO.

Proposed next steps — not completed deliverables
