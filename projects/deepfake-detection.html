01 / WHAT IT IS

THE CONCEPT.

The idea is to compare audio-only, video-only and fused audio/video signals for detecting manipulated media. The current model is an experiment, not a validated or deployable detection product.

HONEST CREDIT The project idea and direction are mine. AI tools generated most of the implementation. My own testing has been limited and the project status is reported accordingly.

02 / CURRENT WORK

WHAT EXISTS.

  • Preprocessed an initial FakeAVCeleb v1.2 set of 941 video clips for an experiment.
  • Created identity-aware training, validation and test partitions in the initial workflow.
  • Trained a multimodal fusion prototype and ran a held-out experimental evaluation.

03 / EVIDENCE & LIMITATIONS

WHAT'S KNOWN.

  • Initial held-out test set: 119 clips; fusion accuracy 97.48% and fusion F1 about 0.976 in that particular experiment.
  • Those numbers describe one dataset-specific experimental split, not real-world detection performance.
  • The full research study, robustness work and broader generalization evaluation remain incomplete; manual verification has been limited.
VISUAL EVIDENCE The illustration on this page is original portfolio artwork, not a screenshot. A verified, permission-cleared product screenshot or recording can be added later.

04 / WHAT COMES NEXT

STILL TO DO.

Proposed next steps — not completed deliverables

  • Document data leakage protections and evaluate demographic, compression and domain-shift limitations.
  • Complete research writing, reproducibility records and final review.
